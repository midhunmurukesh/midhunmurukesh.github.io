About

I am Midhun Murukesh, a PhD candidate and former Prime Minister's Research Fellow (PMRF) at the Climate and Glacier Modelling Lab (Thesis Guide: Dr. Pankaj Kumar, Associate Professor), Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences, Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal.

My research focuses on precipitation downscaling, hydro-climate extremes, and physically interpretable deep learning models—bridging artificial intelligence and climate science to improve regional climate projections over India and beyond.

Research Highlights

Bridging Artificial Intelligence and Climate Science

Regional Climate Impacts

Regional impacts of climate change over India, with a focus on how large-scale change signals translate to local hydro-climate risk.

Precipitation Extremes

Analyzing precipitation extremes under a changing climate using observational, reanalysis, satellite, and CMIP6 datasets.

Explainable AI

Physics-informed machine learning and attribution methods (e.g., Integrated Gradients) to interpret what downscaling models learn.

Super-Resolution Networks

Image super-resolution neural networks (CNNs, GANs) for spatial downscaling and reconstruction of gridded rainfall data.

Transfer & Zero-Shot Learning

Transfer learning, data augmentation, and zero-shot precipitation downscaling across global climate regimes.

Next-Gen Climate Tools

Developing next-generation tools and reproducible Python/Bash workflows for large-scale climate data analysis.
